What the manuals don't cover

Neither Volvo's VIDA nor the LEMON 1994 940 books contain the step that takes the roll-up glass out of the rear door or puts it back in. Every VIDA door-glass document that matches a 940 search is written for a later car (C70 or newer) and is left out here. The glass steps below come from the Volvo Club UK FAQ for the 700/900 series and carry a VOC FAQ tag. Volvo's own glass procedure is in its body manual TP 31298/1, which neither source includes.

Glass

Wear gloves and safety glasses while clearing the broken glass. VOC FAQ The new glass is tempered and can shatter if an edge is struck. VOC FAQ

Parts

Order by VIN. Neither source gives a part number for the glass itself.

  • Rear door glass, left. Ask the dealer or a glass shop whether it comes with the lift channel (the metal rail along its bottom edge). If it doesn't, the old channel moves to the new glass (see Lift channel).
  • Lift channel filler strip, Volvo 1268231. The rubber strip the glass edge presses into inside the lift channel. Bought by length and cut to fit. VOC FAQ
  • Rear door window guide moulding, only if the broken glass damaged it. Up to model year 1994 one part, 3518556-0, fitted both rear doors. Volvo replaced it with sided parts in 1995: 6846392-6 for the left side, 9152002-3 for the right. VIDA bulletin 83007

Door panel off

From the 1994 940 book (window motor removal, rear doors).

  1. Remove the door trim panel. The 940 figure shows what holds it: the lock knob at the top, the speaker cover and the screw beside the speaker, and three press clips along the bottom edge.

  2. Remove the plastic condensation trap (the sheet behind the panel): bend up its retaining clip. Keep it whole; it goes back on.

Get the broken glass out

Volvo Club UK FAQ.

  1. Vacuum the glass out of the heater ducts and the carpet. VOC FAQ

  2. Vacuum inside the door through the openings in the inner panel, all the way down to the bottom. Glass left in the door works its way into the lock mechanism. VOC FAQ

  3. Pull what is left of the old glass out of the lift channel, and any loose pieces from the window guides in the door frame. not from a manual

Lift channel

The glass sits in a metal lift channel. The regulator's lift arms clip to that channel.

  1. Find the clips for the lift arms: the arms end in sliders that ride in the lift channel, and a clip holds each one. The figure is from the 700-series rear-door procedure; compare it with the 940 drawing above before trusting the details. LEMON 700 book

  2. Release the two clips that mount the window to the lift track, and take the channel out. VOC FAQ

  3. If the new glass came with its own channel, skip to New glass.

  4. Moving the old channel to the new glass: pull out the old filler strip and clean the channel. Cut new filler strip 1268231 to the channel's length and lay it in. The glass compresses the strip when it is pushed into the channel. VOC FAQ

  5. The channel has to go back at the same place along the glass's bottom edge, and neither source gives that position. On an intact glass the FAQ says to mark it before removal. Here, ask the glass supplier, or compare against the right rear door's glass. not from a manual

  6. Press the glass into the channel. A glass-shop method from the FAQ: adhesive in the channel, then press the glass in. Let the adhesive cure fully before the window is run. VOC FAQ

New glass in

Volvo Club UK FAQ, "Replacing Window Glass in Track", in reverse.

  1. Put the glass in with the rear edge tilted up and the front edge down, so it clears the tracks, then level it into both side tracks. VOC FAQ

  2. Fit the window into the lift while the lift sits on its plastic guides; the FAQ calls this the easiest way to reinstall it. VOC FAQ

  3. Install the clips for the lift arms. LEMON 700 book

  4. Test function: run the window all the way down and up. LEMON 700 book

If the glass runs crooked or rattles

The FAQ blames dried-out or broken plastic guide blocks in the channel, and, on a 740 driver's door, the red plastic washer between the clip and the slider (p/n 1334889-1). VOC FAQ

Only if the regulator was damaged

700-series rear door window lift mechanism, LEMON 740 book. Skip if the window ran normally before the glass broke.

  1. Disconnect the motor wiring at the connector. Volvo special tool 9996351-4 releases the round pins. LEMON 700 book

  2. Remove the clips for the lift arms, the nuts (block arrows in the figure above), and the lift mechanism. LEMON 700 book

  3. Some doors hold the regulator with rivets instead of nuts: drill them out with a 5/32″ bit; the FAQ fits 6 mm × 1.0 bolts in their place. The assembly comes out through the lower opening in the door. VOC FAQ

  4. Install the lift mechanism, connect the wiring, connect the arms, fit the clips. Fit the nuts but don't tighten them fully. LEMON 700 book

  5. Wind the window up to the stop, adjust the mechanism if needed, then tighten the nuts. Test function. LEMON 700 book

Close up

  1. Refit the condensation trap and bend its retaining clip back down.

  2. Refit the door trim panel: press clips, screw, speaker cover, knob.

  3. Run the window through its full travel once more with the panel on.
